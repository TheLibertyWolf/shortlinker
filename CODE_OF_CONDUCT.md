# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience regardless of age, disability, origin, gender identity or expression,
experience level, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate respectfully and precisely.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Stay focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and never disclose sensitive data.

Insults, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory content, public or private
harassment, and disclosure of information without consent are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately through the channels listed in
the repository security policy. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently exclude a participant according to severity.
